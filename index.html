About the Game

One player. One forest. No way out but through.

NightBound is a single-player psychological horror game about a night walk that goes very wrong. There's no squad to back you up and no NPC to save you — just you, the trees, and whatever's been living in that hut since long before you got here.

The Forest

A hand-crafted woodland that changes shape when you're not looking directly at it. Getting lost is part of the design.

The Hut

The only light for miles. It's warm, it's inviting, and every instinct you have is telling you not to knock.

The Thing Inside

It knows the forest better than you ever will. It's not in a hurry. It doesn't need to be.
